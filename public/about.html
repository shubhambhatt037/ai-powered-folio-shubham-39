the short version

i love building businesses. in 2025, i finished my computer science degree and chose to build my own thing instead of taking a job.

most of what i tried didn't work. i kept shipping anyway. today i build IdeaFast, which turns reddit complaints into clear customer pain reports for founders, and i build software for clients on the side. i share all of it in public on X.

how i got here

2021 – 2025 · learning to build

i studied computer science engineering and fell in love with problem solving: 1000+ leetcode problems and a knight rating. i interned as an SDE at bluestock fintech, where i led a team of 6 and built an IPO information platform and REST API with django, postgres and JWT auth. on the side, i wrote 100+ DSA solution articles for learnyard as a part-time technical writer.

2025 · choosing my own path

everyone around me was chasing a safe job. i didn't. a friend and i started an automation agency: chatbots and n8n workflows. we got 0 clients.

aug 2025 · the first real client

i noticed founders on X asking for MVPs, so we pivoted. within a week i landed a US client from X and shipped a production-ready AI platform for incident management and claims processing: a $4.3K project.

nov 2025 – mar 2026 · the quiet months

i started posting on X and making reels. no new clients for 5 months. i built side projects anyway: Scryb (AI notes that sort themselves), Dictately (free, offline voice typing for mac) and ChannelMRR. most never launched. i learned from every one of them.

feb – jun 2026 · IdeaFast

in february i got the idea: see what people actually complain about, before you build. in april an n8n automation for a game studio paid ~$100, my first client money in months. in may i shipped my first site with a buy button. it started as a thin ChatGPT wrapper, so i rebuilt it to read real reddit data. in june i made my first online sale: $5. it took 3.5 months to reach my bank.

aug – nov 2026 · momentum

i started freelancing in AI training, reviewing the data AI models learn from. it funds IdeaFast while it grows. in september GummySearch listed IdeaFast on its alternatives page and revenue crossed $100. in november i made $68 in a single day, twice, and shipped weekly watch and the first monthly reddit pain report.

now

IdeaFast has made $413 in verified revenue. next goal: $1,000. still shipping every week, in public.
